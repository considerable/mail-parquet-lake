Execution health ≠ data freshness

A successful run does not prove that every email was synchronized or that the report used fresh data. The executive view above uses application checkpoints and inventory; workflow success alone does not establish completeness.

Public by design

Only workflow metadata and approved aggregate executive evidence are published. No email content, reports, raw logs, credentials or private snapshots are published. The executive artifact is explicitly projected and validated. Detailed job steps cover the latest 30 runs.

Know the blind spots

Refreshes after operational workflows finish and roughly every 30 minutes. GitHub schedules can be delayed. The Dashboard workflow can itself be disabled by inactivity. Stale warnings are evaluated in your browser. Independent CloudWatch heartbeat and source alarms remain private; no notification subscription is configured.
