Execution health ≠ data freshness

A successful run does not prove that every email was synchronized or that the report used fresh data. Application checkpoints and output validation are not instrumented yet.

Public by design

Only workflow metadata is published. No email content, reports, raw logs, credentials, or job artifacts are collected. Detailed job steps cover the latest 30 runs.

Know the blind spots

Refreshes after operational workflows finish and roughly every 30 minutes. GitHub schedules can be delayed. The Dashboard workflow can itself be disabled by inactivity. Its stale-snapshot warning is evaluated by this page; independent missed-run alerting is not enabled.
